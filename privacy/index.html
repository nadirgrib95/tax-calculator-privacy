Local storage and optional cloud sync / Stockage local et cloud facultatif

WorkSlip works offline and saves records on your device. When you create an account and enable sync, we use Supabase in Canada (ca-central-1) to store your account email, user identifier, business profile and logo, clients and their contact details, products, invoices and amounts, scheduled work, notes and completed time sessions. This supports backups across devices and future desktop access. Running timers and unfinished form drafts remain on the device. Network providers may process connection and security logs, including IP addresses.

WorkSlip fonctionne hors ligne et enregistre sur votre appareil. Avec un compte et la synchronisation activée, Supabase conserve au Canada votre courriel, identifiant, profil et logo, coordonnées clients, produits, factures et montants, travaux, notes et sessions terminées pour l’accès sur plusieurs appareils. Les chronomètres actifs et brouillons restent locaux. Les fournisseurs peuvent traiter des journaux de connexion et sécurité, dont l’adresse IP.

Purpose and security / Finalité et sécurité

We use account and business data to authenticate you, synchronize records and provide the app’s functions. Access rules restrict cloud records to your account; transfers use HTTPS. Local storage relies on operating-system protections and is not separately encrypted by WorkSlip. Protect your device and credentials. WorkSlip includes no advertising, advertising identifiers or behavioural analytics and does not sell your records. Supabase is a service provider; see its privacy policy. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process access logs under GitHub’s policy.

Les données servent à l’authentification, synchronisation et fonctions de l’application. L’accès cloud est limité à votre compte; transferts HTTPS. Le stockage local dépend des protections du système sans chiffrement supplémentaire par WorkSlip. Protégez l’appareil et vos identifiants. Aucune publicité ni analyse comportementale; aucune vente de vos données.

Retention and deletion / Conservation et suppression

Records remain locally until you remove them or uninstall. Cloud records remain until you delete them with sync enabled or delete your account. In Settings → Account, sync & backup → Delete account, you can permanently delete your account and its cloud records, along with this device’s account cache and private drafts. Export anything you need first. Offline record deletions are queued until reconnection. Deleted-record markers may remain to synchronize deletions, without the removed record content. Uninstalling does not delete the cloud account. Other devices, backups and shared copies must be removed separately. Account deletion does not cancel store subscriptions. Purchase providers may retain transaction and fraud-prevention records as legally required.

You can also request account deletion without the app by emailing nadirgrib95@gmail.com from your account email with subject “WorkSlip account deletion”. We will verify ownership, delete the account and cloud business records, and reply once completed; we aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve requests or meet legal obligations. Do not send passwords or unnecessary client records.

Les données restent jusqu’à suppression; le cloud reste jusqu’à suppression synchronisée ou suppression du compte. Réglages → Compte, synchronisation et sauvegarde → Supprimer le compte supprime le compte et ses données cloud ainsi que le cache et brouillons sur cet appareil. Les suppressions hors ligne attendent la connexion. Désinstaller ne supprime pas le compte et ne résilie pas l’abonnement. Supprimez séparément les copies partagées et sauvegardes. Sans l’application, contactez nadirgrib95@gmail.com depuis le courriel du compte avec l’objet « WorkSlip account deletion ». Après vérification, nous visons une suppression sous 30 jours. Ne partagez pas votre mot de passe.
